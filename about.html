Corrie

I specialize in cats.

This is Corrie's Pet Grooming. I work at 4595 Harlan Street in Wheat Ridge. I specialize in cats. I also take dogs, rabbits, and guinea pigs.

Difficult dogs and cats come through the door. I handle them without sedation. Bath, brush, a haircut or trim, ears, nails — including ingrown and broken — and teeth brushing.

Call if you want a time. Walk-in nail trims run Tuesday–Saturday 9–12.
